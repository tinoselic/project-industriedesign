Mario Selić

Office Augsburg

Mario Selić founded his office Selić Industriedesign in Augsburg, Germany, in 1999 after gaining extensive experience in the design of consumer and investment goods—particularly in robotics and automation processes. With over 35 years of professional experience, his work focuses on the development of high-quality, long-lasting products with a responsible approach to resource use, principles he primarily implemented during his time with the KUKA Robotic Team.

Mario studied physics and mathematics at the Ludwig-Maximilians-University Munich and later pursued industrial design at the Munich University of Applied Sciences. In 1997, he was appointed as a professor in the field of investment goods at the prestigious International Graduate School of Industrial Design in Seoul, South Korea.

His contributions to the field have earned him numerous international design awards. Since 2019, he has served as a jury member for several leading design competitions, including the Red Dot Award: Product Design in Essen, Red Dot Award: Design Concept in Singapore, Contemporary Good Design Award in Xiamen, China, and the iF Design Award.

Tino Selić

Office Helsinki

Tino Selić studied Interactive Media (B.A.) at Augsburg University of Applied Sciences and is currently completing his M.A. in New Media at Aalto University in Espoo, Finland. As a versatile designer and creative developer, his expertise spans a wide range of creative and technical disciplines—from 3D modeling and animation to web development and interactive audiovisual media experiences.

He often integrates insights from large language models (LLMs) into the design process, yet approaches technology with both sensitivity and critical awareness—emphasizing the ethical dimensions of automation and advocating for design practices that consider both human and non-human actors.

Through his perspective and skill set, Tino expands Selić Industriedesign’s range of services and brings fresh inspiration and innovative approaches to future product developments.
