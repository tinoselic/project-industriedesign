Bosch TASSIMO TAS43


Heißgetränk-Vollautomat


The Bosch TAS43 hot drink machine appears in the timeless modern design.

A highly brilliantly running around front surface, high grade chromium-plated design applications and an integrated water tank round the appearance harmoniously. The cup stand is height adjustable, so that even 15 cm high latte macchiato glasses fit easily under it.

Selić Industriedesign service: CAD design support, CAD freeform modeling and design refinement, processing of design data for the engineering department
